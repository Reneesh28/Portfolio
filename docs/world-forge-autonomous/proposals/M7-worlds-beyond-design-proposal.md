## 2. The Convergence (M7.2)
**Idea in one line:** a round meeting-place of six gates. Each gate stands at the edge of the Convergence facing its territory, and carries that territory's colour. A tall compass monolith stands at the centre.

- **The island:** the existing Convergence island from P2. A circular stone plaza, about 40 m across, is ringed by **six gates**, simple stone arches, each lit in its territory's colour and turned toward its island. The golden links already run from the plaza to each territory.
- **The compass monolith:** a tall dark stone at the centre with six faint coloured lines on its faces, one pointing at each gate. It replaces the P2 monolith and standing stones.
- **Arrival:** the camera settles above and behind the plaza, so the six gates and the territories floating around it all read.
- **The card:** "The Worlds Beyond", with the six territories as buttons.
- **Choosing a territory:**
  - the camera flies to it and settles on its landmark;
  - the territory's panel opens;
  - "Return to the Convergence" brings the camera back.

  Previous/next walk the territories.
- **Optional by design** (master doc): scrolling continues to the Horizon at any time, and the bottom navigation and World Map work throughout. There are no collectibles, completion marks or mini-games.
